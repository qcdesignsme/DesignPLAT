✉️ A note to the team

Dear PLAT team,

I live in Baltimore, and I've spent my career on the part of public services most people never see: the steps between "I need this" and "it's done." At VA.gov I co-ran trauma-informed research with Veterans filing mental health claims. At farmers.gov I interviewed USDA field and program staff to learn why producers arrived at conservation visits unprepared, then proposed a step-by-step tool to fix it upstream. At Mentor Collective I mapped journeys from 100+ participants and rebuilt a staff tool that had a 50%+ error rate.

Permitting is that same kind of problem. Applicants don't know what's required or where their request stands. Reviewers spend time chasing missing documents. Each agency sees only its own part of the process. I want to help map it with the people who live it, test small fixes, and hand DoIT prototypes they can build from.

As a first-generation college graduate, I know what it costs when a process assumes you already know the rules. Making those rules legible is the work I want to do for Maryland.

Thank you for reading. I'd welcome the chance to talk.

Qian Chen
