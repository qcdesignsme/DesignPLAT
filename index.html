✉️ A note to the team

Dear PLAT team,

I live in Baltimore, and I care about how this city works for the people in it, from volunteering at the Maryland SPCA to supporting local farms that tackle food insecurity.

Our housing crisis is not just about supply and affordability, but also about slow, confusing processes. When I read Governor Moore's executive order creating PLAT, I saw work I've been doing for years: mapping complicated processes with the people using them.

I've spent 10+ years in design, from start-ups to government, including projects with USDA, the National Institutes of Health and the VA. At USDA, I interviewed field staff to learn why farmers arrived at conservation visits unprepared, then redesigned the process upstream. At the VA, I ran trauma-informed research with Veterans filing disability claims.

I work end to end, from discovery research and journey mapping to design systems and accessibility (508 and WCAG 2.2). I also build AI into team workflows, including the interactive prototypes I hand to engineers.

Permitting needs that approach: understanding the problem beyond the screen, then testing prototypes with applicants and staff until the solution works at scale. I'd love to bring that to multifamily housing and the projects behind it.

Thank you for reading. I'd welcome the chance to talk.

Qian Chen
